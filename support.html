Getting started

Cape Fear Shark Tooth Explorer is a field reference and inspection tool for fossil shark teeth of the Cape Fear region. Open the Shark Tooth Lab for 3D inspection, the Identify section to compare a photo against reference material, and the Notebook to record finds, measurements, and expedition notes.

The 3D tooth lab

  • Choose a species, then a finish to see how preservation and colour vary.
  • Drag to rotate, use two fingers to zoom, and use the vertical orbit control to inspect the root and cusplets.
  • Reset View returns the model to its default orientation.
  • Full-screen mode gives the model more room on smaller devices.
  • If motion makes you uncomfortable, turn on Reduce Motion in iOS Settings; the app respects it.

Identifying a tooth from a photo

Choose a clear, well-lit photo against a plain background, with the full crown and root visible. Comparison is human-led: the app presents reference views side by side with your photo and your measurements. It does not declare an identification for you. Treat results as a guide and verify against a museum or specialist reference before relying on them.

Permissions and dictation

  • Camera, Photos, Microphone, Speech Recognition, and Location are all optional. Each is requested only when you first use the related feature.
  • If you declined a permission, restore it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, or in Settings → Cape Fear Shark Tooth Explorer.
  • Dictation requires on-device speech recognition. On devices or languages where it is unavailable, dictation stays off — typing always works.
  • Location is used only while you are using the app, to label records and build GPX routes.

Your data and privacy

Notes, photos, measurements, and locations stay on your device. There is no account and no server receiving them. Deleting an entry removes it from the app; removing the app removes its local container. Exports and device backups are separate and are managed by you.

Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Exporting records

Exports are started by you and go wherever you send them. GPX exports contain the coordinates you recorded, so review a file before sharing it. If an export appears empty, confirm that the entry you selected actually has a saved location.

Purchases and refunds

The app is a one-time purchase with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Apple handles all billing. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; we cannot issue refunds directly. If a purchase does not appear on a second device, confirm that both devices use the same Apple Account.

Requirements

The app is built for iPhone and iPad. 3D inspection and the on-device Field Guide need a device that supports the relevant Apple frameworks; where a capability is unavailable, the app disables that feature rather than degrading silently.

Reporting a problem

If something is wrong — a model that will not load, a crash, a permission prompt that never appears, or text that reads incorrectly — email bradturner@seaglasslabsllc.org with the details above. Reports about identification accuracy are especially welcome, with the photo and the reference you compared against.
