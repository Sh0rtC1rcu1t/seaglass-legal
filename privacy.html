Privacy Policy — Cape Fear Shark Tooth Explorer

SeaGlass Labs LLC · Effective October 25, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0

In short

  • We do not collect your data. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • SeaGlass Labs LLC operates no server that receives your notes, photos, or locations.
  • No advertising, no cross-app tracking, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • What you record stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

1. Who we are

Cape Fear Shark Tooth Explorer is published by SeaGlass Labs LLC, Wilmington, North Carolina, United States. For any privacy question, contact bradturner@seaglasslabsllc.org. This policy applies to the iPhone and iPad app distributed on the App Store under bundle identifier com.seaglasslabs.CapeFearSharkToothExplorer, including all of its features.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, sell, rent, or share personal information. Specifically, the app:

  • does not create an account or ask for your name, email address, or phone number;
  • does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • does not build a profile of you or your usage, and does not use advertising identifiers;
  • does not transmit your notebook entries, photographs, measurements, or locations to us or to any third party.

The app contains no networking code of its own. The only features that use the network are Apple system services described in section 7, which are provided by Apple under Apple's terms.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its own private container on your device. It is not uploaded to us.

  • Field records — notebook entries, observations, measurements, comparison results, and corrections you make.
  • Photographs you choose — kept so you can compare and annotate them. The comparison working copy is re-encoded with camera, date, and location metadata removed.
  • Locations you record — find coordinates and expedition routes you explicitly capture.
  • Exports you create — files you generate, such as GPX routes or image exports.
  • A local signing key — a key held in the iOS Keychain, used to sign your own review records so you can tell whether a record changed after you saved it. It never leaves the device.

This data is protected by iOS data protection and the app sandbox. Other apps cannot read it unless you deliberately export or share it. Deleting the app removes its container files. Files you exported, and any device backup you have enabled, are managed separately by you and by Apple.

4. Permissions

Every permission below is optional. You are asked only when you first use the related feature, and you can change any of them at any time in iOS Settings. Declining a permission disables that feature; the rest of the app keeps working.

PermissionWhy it is usedWhat happens
Camera Photograph a tooth for private measurement, comparison, and augmented-reality viewing. Capture happens on your device. Images stay in the app unless you export them.
Photo Library Choose an existing photo for identification and to attach to a notebook entry. Only the photo you select is read. A working copy is made with camera and location metadata removed before comparison.
Microphone and Speech Recognition Optional dictation, so you can speak field notes instead of typing. Recognition runs on your device and only when on-device recognition is available. The app does not send microphone audio to a server, and does not fall back to a server when on-device recognition is unavailable — dictation is simply unavailable and typing still works.
Location (while using the app) Record where you found a tooth and the route of an expedition. Location is used to label records you create and to build GPX exports. It is never sent to us. The app does not request background location.

5. On-device intelligence

The Field Guide feature can use Apple's on-device system language model (available on supported devices running iOS 26 or later). That model runs locally. Your questions, your notes, and the generated responses are not sent to SeaGlass Labs LLC. If the model is not available on your device, the feature is unavailable rather than being routed to a server.

6. Purchases

The app is a one-time paid purchase and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple processes the payment. We receive sales and proceeds reports from Apple that do not include your name, payment card, or billing address.

7. Apple services

Some features rely on Apple frameworks that communicate with Apple independently of the app:

  • Apple Maps — used to display maps and coordinates. Map data is requested from Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy policy.
  • App Store and TestFlight — distribution, purchase, and any feedback you send through TestFlight are handled by Apple.
  • Device backup — if you enable iCloud Backup or computer backups, the operating system may include app files in that backup according to your settings.

We do not control these services and do not receive the data they handle. Apple's privacy policy governs them.

8. Exports and sharing

Exports and shares are always started by you. Once a file leaves the app, the destination you chose — another app, a person, or a cloud service — handles its own copy under its own terms. Exported GPX files contain the coordinates you recorded, so review a file before you share it.

9. Data retention

We retain nothing, because we collect nothing. On-device data remains until you delete the individual records or delete the app.

10. Your choices

  • Change or revoke any permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, or in Settings → Cape Fear Shark Tooth Explorer.
  • Delete individual notebook entries inside the app.
  • Delete all local app data by removing the app from your device.
  • Ask us a privacy question at bradturner@seaglasslabsllc.org.

11. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The App Store age rating shown on the product page describes the content of the app.

12. Security

Because your records stay on your device, their security depends mainly on your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS data protection. The app uses the iOS sandbox, platform data protection, and a Keychain-held signing key. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device protected and your operating system up to date.

13. International users

We do not transfer personal data internationally, because we do not collect it. Data you create remains on your own device, wherever you are.

14. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and change the effective date above. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes. The current version of this policy is always the one published at this address.
